Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you join.

Is the group free to join?

Yes. It’s 100% free. Just click “Join” and answer a few quick questions so we can keep the community high-quality.

How do you handle sensitive topics like politics or religion?

We encourage diverse perspectives while enforcing respectful dialogue. Personal attacks, hate speech, and misinformation are moderated.

What can I post?

Funny, political, religious, social tips, personal stories—if it’s civil, constructive, and follows the rules, it’s welcome.

Can I promote my page or products?

Limited promos on designated threads only. We’re community-first. Please check the group rules after joining.
